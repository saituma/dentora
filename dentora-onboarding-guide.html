Setting Up Your
AI Receptionist

Everything your practice needs to go live with Dentora. Complete the required steps to activate your AI receptionist — optional items improve accuracy and are strongly recommended.

Steps 1 – 6   Practice information
01
Clinic Profile Required
The basic identity of your practice — used in patient-facing communications and throughout your dashboard.
Practice name Address Phone number Email address Timezone
02
Knowledge Base — Services, FAQs & Staff Required
Your AI receptionist draws on this to answer questions about treatments, pricing, team members, and policies. Add at least one service and one FAQ to proceed.
Service name Category Duration (mins) Price (£) Description FAQ question FAQ answer FAQ category Staff name & role T&C document (PDF / DOCX / TXT · max 5 MB)
03
Voice & Tone Required
Choose your AI receptionist's voice, greeting script, speaking speed, and personality. A live preview plays before you save.
Voice selection Greeting message Tone Speaking speed Language
04
Phone Number Required
Select a UK phone number for your AI receptionist from the available list. This is the number patients will call.
Select number from list
05
Google Calendar Integration OAuth setup
Authorise Dentora to read and write your Google Calendar so the AI can check real-time availability and book appointments. Completed via Google sign-in — no file upload required.
Google account email Calendar ID (optional)
06
Opening Hours & Schedule Required
Set your weekly operating hours, break windows, and any closed dates. At least one working day must be enabled to proceed.
Working days Open / close times Break windows Closed dates
Before you start — have these ready
  • A full services list with treatment names, durations, and prices for both NHS and private treatments.
  • Your most common patient questions — "Do you take NHS?", "What are your emergency slots?" etc.
  • Your weekly opening hours including any half-days or regular lunch breaks.
  • The Google account that owns your practice calendar — you'll authorise it in step 5.
  • A CSV export from your current patient management system — you'll upload this in step 8.

Steps 7 – 10   AI training, patient import, testing & go-live
07
AI Context — Practice Documents Optional · Recommended
Upload documents about your practice so the AI gives richer, more accurate answers. Up to 10 files, 5 MB each. You can also paste information or a website URL directly into the AI chat window.
Treatment brochures (PDF / DOCX) Price lists (XLSX / CSV) Policy documents (PDF / DOCX / TXT) Custom FAQ document (TXT) Clinic photo (JPG / PNG · max 1.5 MB)
08
Import Patient Records Recommended
Export your existing patient list from your current practice software and upload it as a CSV file. Dentora imports all records so the AI recognises returning callers by name from day one — no waiting for data to build up organically. Max 5,000 rows · 10 MB. See the column reference below.
Full name (or First + Last columns) Phone number Date of birth Last visit date Notes / medical flags CSV file · max 10 MB · 5,000 rows
09
Test Call Interactive
Make a live test call to your AI receptionist before going live. Verify it answers correctly, books appointments, and handles edge cases. Nothing to prepare or upload.
10
Publish & Go Live Required
Review your full configuration and publish. Your AI receptionist activates immediately. Every setting can be changed at any time from your dashboard without any downtime.
