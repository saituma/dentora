// 04 · How It Works

A patient calls. Here is exactly what happens.

The entire process — from first ring to confirmed appointment — typically takes under two minutes. No staff involvement is required.

Step 1 — Instant answer

Dentora picks up in under one second

The patient hears your practice name spoken by a clear, friendly AI voice — no hold music, no ringing out, no robot menus. Dentora says something like: "Good morning, Pearl Dental. How can I help you today?" and the conversation begins immediately.

Step 2 — Listen and understand

The AI identifies exactly what the patient needs

The patient speaks naturally. They might say "I need to book a filling" or "I'm in pain and need to come in today" or "Can you tell me how much a hygiene appointment costs?" Dentora understands the intent behind what they say — not just the keywords — and responds accordingly.

It handles accents, incomplete sentences, background noise, and topic changes mid-conversation — just as a human receptionist would.

Step 3 — Check real-time availability

Live calendar access — no double-bookings, ever

For booking requests, Dentora connects to your Google Calendar in real time. It sees exactly which slots are free, offers suitable options to the patient, and confirms the chosen time. It will never offer a slot that is already taken.

Step 4 — Confirm and record

Booking appears in your calendar immediately

Once the patient agrees a time, the appointment is created in your calendar on the spot. The patient receives a confirmation. An AI-written summary of the call — what was discussed, what was booked, any notes — is emailed to your team and stored in your dashboard.

Step 5 — Escalate when needed

Dentora knows its limits

If a call involves something sensitive — a complaint, a complex clinical question, or an emergency requiring immediate human response — Dentora flags it instantly for your team. No patient is left without a resolution path. Your staff receive the full context of the conversation so they can follow up with complete information.

Step 6 — Staff review

You stay in complete control

Every call — whether it ended in a booking, an escalation, or an FAQ answer — is available in your dashboard. Staff can read the transcript, listen to the recording, amend any booking, or mark calls for follow-up. Nothing is hidden and nothing is final until your team is satisfied.

// 09 · AI Voice Technology

The technology behind the voice — explained simply.

You do not need to understand how Dentora works to use it. But for those who want to know, here is a plain-English explanation.

Listening — speech recognition

When a patient speaks, Dentora converts their voice into text using advanced speech recognition — the same technology used by smartphone voice assistants, but trained specifically on UK accents and dental terminology.

It handles background noise, regional accents, hesitations ("um", "erm"), and incomplete sentences. The recognition happens in real time, so the response is immediate — there is no obvious "processing" pause.

Understanding — the AI brain

Once the speech is converted to text, a large language model (the same category of technology as widely-publicised AI assistants) works out what the patient means — not just the words they said, but the intent behind them.

This is why Dentora can handle "I need to come in — my tooth is killing me" as an emergency, even though the word "emergency" was never said.

Responding — natural voice synthesis

Dentora's response is converted back into speech using neural voice technology. The voice is configured during your setup — you choose the name, accent, and tone that best represents your practice. It sounds natural, warm, and human.

Multiple providers, always available

Dentora does not rely on a single AI provider. If one system has an outage, Dentora automatically switches to a backup provider in milliseconds — meaning your phone is never down due to a third-party issue.

Custom voice & persona

On the Growth and Enterprise plans, you can configure a custom AI persona — a name, a personality, a specific greeting style. Your AI receptionist can be "Sophie at Oakfield Dental" rather than a generic assistant.

// 11 · Security & Data Protection

Patient data protected to the highest standard.

Patient data is among the most sensitive information that exists. Dentora is engineered — not just configured — to protect it. Every architectural decision puts data security first.

🔒

AES-256 encryption at rest

Every patient name, phone number, date of birth, and clinical note stored by Dentora is encrypted using AES-256-GCM — the same standard used by UK high street banks. Even if someone obtained a copy of the database, the data would be completely unreadable without the encryption keys.

🌐

Encrypted in transit

All data moving between your browser, the Dentora servers, and any integrated systems is encrypted using TLS 1.3 — the strongest current standard. No data travels over the internet in a readable form.

🇬🇧

Data stays in the UK

All Dentora infrastructure is hosted in the United Kingdom. Patient data is never transferred to servers in other countries. You remain the data controller at all times, in full compliance with UK GDPR and ICO requirements.

👤

Multi-factor authentication

All staff accounts require two-factor authentication — a password plus a time-based one-time code from an authenticator app (the same method used by most banking apps). A stolen password alone cannot access patient data.

📋

Tamper-proof audit log

Every access to patient data, every booking, every staff action, and every configuration change is recorded in a write-once audit log with a precise timestamp and the identity of the user responsible. This log cannot be edited or deleted.

🏢

Complete tenant isolation

It is architecturally impossible for one practice's data to be seen by another. Each practice exists in a completely separate data partition. This is enforced in the code — not just in access permissions.

Automatic data retention enforcement

📼

Call transcripts: 2 years

Retained for the standard clinical records period, then permanently deleted automatically. No manual housekeeping required.

📁

Audit logs: 7 years

Retained for 7 years in line with NHS data governance guidance for healthcare records, then automatically removed.
